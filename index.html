ABOUT OUR COMPANY

Printing Experience You Can Build On.

Established in 2001, Animesh Graphics Engineers is a Ghaziabad-based printing company serving business printing requirements across a range of applications.

Our product range includes self-adhesive labels, product labels, plain labels, stickers, booklets, brochures, warranty cards, instruction manuals and mono-cartons.

We also offer specialised printed products such as polycarbonate stickers, polyester and special film labels, decal stickers, transfer stickers and control panel stickers.

OUR CAPABILITIES

Printing Services

OFFSET PRINTING

Offset Printing

High-quality, consistent, and cost-effective printing for large-volume requirements. Our offset printing solutions deliver sharp colours, precise details, and reliable quality, making them ideal for manuals, warranty cards, booklets, brochures, inserts, and commercial print applications.

DIGITAL PRINTING

Digital Printing

Fast, flexible, and cost-effective printing for short-run requirements and immediate production. Digital printing enables quick turnaround with consistent quality, making it ideal for urgent jobs, variable data, prototypes, samples, personalized prints, and low-volume requirements.

FLEXOGRAPHIC PRINTING

Flexographic Printing

High-speed, efficient printing for continuous roll-to-roll production and high-volume requirements. Flexographic printing delivers consistent quality and precise registration, making it ideal for labels, stickers, packaging materials, films, and other roll-form applications.

SCREEN PRINTING

Screen Printing

Durable and versatile printing for specialized applications and demanding surfaces. Screen printing delivers excellent ink coverage, opacity, and durability, making it ideal for polycarbonate labels, industrial graphics, control panels, stickers, and specialty print applications.
