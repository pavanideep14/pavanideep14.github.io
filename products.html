PRINTED LABELS

Clear Product Identification With Professional Presentation

Printed product labels provide an important point of identification between a product and the customer. They can communicate product information, branding, handling details, identification information and other required content in a clear and professional format.

Thermal Transfer Ribbons

Thermal transfer ribbons used with compatible thermal transfer printing systems for producing variable information such as barcodes, product identification and coding information.

THERMAL TRANSFER RIBBONS

Reliable Variable-Data Printing Support

Thermal transfer ribbons are consumable printing materials used with compatible thermal transfer printers.

They are commonly used for printing variable information such as barcodes, product identification, batch details, serial numbers and other changing information.

The appropriate ribbon depends on the printer, label material, application and required print characteristics.

What Are Thermal Transfer Ribbons?

A thermal transfer ribbon is a coated material used in a thermal transfer printing process. During printing, the required information is transferred from the ribbon onto a compatible label or printing surface.

They are commonly used where variable information needs to be printed on demand rather than being permanently pre-printed.

Typical Uses

  • Barcode printing
  • Product identification
  • Batch and lot information
  • Serial numbers
  • Variable product information
  • Inventory identification

Advantages

  • Suitable for variable-data printing
  • Useful for on-demand information
  • Supports barcode applications
  • Useful for identification systems
  • Works with compatible thermal transfer printers

Where Are Thermal Transfer Ribbons Used?

Thermal transfer ribbons are commonly used in manufacturing, warehousing, logistics, inventory control, retail and other operations requiring variable information printing.

Choosing the Appropriate Ribbon

Ribbon selection should consider the printer being used, label material, application, required print quality and other operational requirements.

The correct ribbon specification helps achieve suitable printing performance for the intended application.
